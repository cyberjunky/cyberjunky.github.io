CyberJunky's Blog is a personal tech blog by Ron Klinkien. Topics include home automation with Home Assistant, KNX bus systems, solar power monitoring, electronics, and whatever else ends up on the workbench.

The site is statically generated with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) — no server, no database, no subscription needed. Everything is free to read.

For the personal story behind the blog, see the [About me](/about-me/) page.
